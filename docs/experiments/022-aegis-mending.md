# Experiment 022 — Aegis mending

Depth 60 makes Aegis mending available to newly generated Clerics. Existing characters/applicants keep their techniques. The Cleric first heals the living ally with lowest health percentage (party-order ties), then each unused healing point grants one shield to every living party member. Each recipient uses the existing 60%-maximum-HP storage cap; wasted grants are excluded from caster totals. Aegis heals at 2.25 attack power every five seconds versus standard Mending light's 2.5: a 10% reduction. It cleanses with Infirmary rank 1 and never revives. No unused healing means no ward.

This composes healing and ward tags with existing effect primitives. Ritual focus applies once, despite matching both tags. Dawn shifts the first cast to 1s without altering later recurrence; early healing can now become protection. Casting later in party order means preceding reflected hits can consume healing before overflow, and a second ordinary Cleric may restore health between Aegis casts. Warden still grants reliable wards regardless of health, every four seconds. Aegis is a conditional hybrid with stronger Cleric attack power, rather than a replacement for Warden's role.

`node tools/aegis-study.js --single-target` evaluates the first prototype: unused healing shields only the healed ally, with scales 1.5/1.75/2 and conversions 0.5/1. `022-aegis-single-target-results.json` preserves 19,440 encounters. Every prototype improves zero win-rate groups against standard healing and loses 12–15. Proactive shielding on the front line did not justify losing healing in these samples.

`node tools/aegis-study.js` evaluates party overflow, scales 1.75/2/2.25 and conversions 0.5/1, against standard/Quiet/Greater reference casts. Another 19,440 encounters in `022-aegis-results.json`: three compositions (GRC, GRCW, GRCC), depths 65/75/85/95, levels 16/24/32, no equipment/ritual/Dawn, twenty paired seeds. Only the first Cleric changes; other support uses ritual and the Ranger steel, facility ranks 2/2. All terminal saves validate. Fixed starting actor fields and seed do not force identical critical sequences once casualties/timing differ.

Selected 2.25/1 improves six of 108 composition/depth/level/gear groups against standard healing, loses eight and ties 94. Against the best standard/Quiet/Greater choice in the same gear, it improves four, loses eleven and ties 93. Most ties are all-win or all-loss cohorts. With GRC at level 32 depth 75 and Dawn, Aegis wins 4/20 versus standard 0/20; with GRCW at level 24 depth 75 without equipment, Aegis wins 17/20 versus standard 20/20. Cohorts are small and do not establish significance or general superiority. No change is made to the older Cleric techniques or Warden balance.

170 tests pass. New cases cover gated reproducible generation, unchanged existing applicants, partial/full/zero overflow, party caps, missing-health priority, dead members, cleanse, reflected damage absorption, caster/recipient attribution, ritual's single factor, Dawn recurrence, entering/report snapshots, legacy unrecorded grants, premature save rejection and exact four-hour live/offline recovery parity. The former throughput-budget test remains specific to pure-effect variants; Aegis deliberately trades direct healing strength for a conditional second effect, tested separately. Early progression simulation results stay unchanged.
