# Experiment 023 — Unequal-level Cleric preparation

`node tools/aegis-recruit-study.js` compares Cleric replacement and a fourth Cleric in real mixed-investment journey states at 600/3,600/14,400 seconds. Only states that actually reached depth 60 are eligible (22/30 early, all 30 later). Paired standard/Quiet/Greater/Aegis applicants reuse a generated Cleric's identity, origin, talent, level and progression. Technique is controlled; the study is conditional on finding that applicant and excludes the random search cost. Original parties are Guardian/Ranger/standard Cleric, with continuous facility spending and manual ten-win recovery at the conquered frontier.

Each strategy uses actual Gold purchases: fresh hire; ritual commission/hire; hall/hire/affordable mentoring; ritual plus mentoring; or desk/survey/Dawn commission/hall/hire/mentoring. Fourth-slot cases also buy quarters. Nothing grants missing currency, levels or a blueprint. Dawn surveys earn normal rewards and XP for the original party, so those cases also take preparation time and may reach a higher mentor ceiling. Report gross purchase spending separately from net treasury spending after survey rewards. Unaffordable strategies are recorded as ineligible rather than silently subsidized.

Each prepared state either pushes one frontier room or follows actual successive pushes through the next Gatekeeper. There is no fabricated depth jump: intermediate rooms pay Gold/XP and recovery. Automatic repeats stop on a defeat or the destination. Every terminal state validates. Raw data: `023-aegis-recruit-results.json`, preserving 5,760 eligible routes (9,776 actual encounters) plus failed purchase eligibility records. These are controlled actively managed snapshots, not proof of unattended progression or player enjoyment.

In the one-hour sources, a fresh Cleric averages level 25.7 versus veterans 104.5. Aegis replacement wins 13/30 single frontier rooms, but only 1/30 routes through the next Gatekeeper. Standard replacement wins 13/30 single rooms and 1/30 Gatekeeper routes; adding ritual gives standard 3/30 versus Aegis 1/30 Gatekeeper routes. Thus “won the next ordinary room” understates replacement risk. Mentored Aegis replacement reaches mean level 97.87 and succeeds 30/30 Gatekeeper routes, spending 14,317.13 Gold on average including hire/hall/training. The paid level gap matters much more than the technique in this cohort.

Adding a fresh fourth Cleric retains the veteran healer: both standard and Aegis succeed 30/30 single-room and Gatekeeper routes in the one-hour cohort, at mean 372 Gold including quarters/hire. Mentoring improves some times but is not needed for these victories. Aegis does not uniquely cause the fourth-slot advantage. At four-hour sources, fresh Aegis replacement succeeds 16/30 next rooms; mentored replacement 26/30, ritual+mentor 24/30, funded Dawn+mentor 28/30. Through the next Gatekeeper, fresh Aegis replacement succeeds 0/30, mentoring 21/30, ritual+mentoring 17/30 and funded Dawn+mentoring 26/30. Each fresh fourth-slot strategy succeeds 30/30. Later budgets and eligibility differ from early ones; averages should not be compared as fixed-budget skill rankings.

No class, technique or mentorship price changes follow. Added an expandable **Combat after quoted training** preview to applicant preparation quotes. It uses the same combat-description function as roster inspection, at the quoted target level, current Guild ranks and explicitly no equipment. Cost, hiring and mentoring remain separate actions. This helps players assess the quoted future healing/ward damage rather than comparing a current novice's ability output against a veteran.

170 tests pass; engine balance is unchanged. Browser previews naturally generated Elf Aegis Cleric Lethra Thornsong: level 7/27 power,85 hire+1,113 mentoring=1,198 Gold to level 28. Before purchase, the preview shows 67 basic damage and up to 151 healing or 151 shield per living ally every 5s, cleanse, caps and conditional overflow. Opening remains 5s because equipment is excluded. No warnings/errors; current roster/party/plans stay preserved, with Lethra still an applicant. Refreshes cost the normal 10 Gold.
